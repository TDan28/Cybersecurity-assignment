***Q7. Give one example of cybersecurity risk.***  

An external attacker could execute a phishing attack (threat) by exploiting weak faculty passwords or active accounts of former employees (vulnerability), resulting in unauthorized access and theft of sensitive passport copies and student records (impact).  

​***Q8. What is an attack surface?**  

An attack surface is the total sum of all possible points, pathways, and entry ways (such as internet-facing systems, accounts, software, and hardware) where an unauthorized user or attacker can try to enter or extract data from a network.  

​***Q9. Identify at least five parts of the university's attack surface.***  

​1)The Internet-accessible student portal (exposed public web interface).  

​2)Computers running outdated software/operating systems (not updated for 6 months).  

​3)Faculty user accounts with weak passwords.  

​4)Active employee accounts belonging to former staff.  

​5)Email systems receiving external messages (targeted by phishing/suspicious password-reset emails).  

​6)Network-connected databases storing student records, passport copies, and research data.
